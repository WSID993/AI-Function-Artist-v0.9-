AFA GRAPH 0.8 — Help

Graph equations

  • y = x^2
  • y = sin(x)
  • y = 1/x
  • y = sqrt(x)

Mathematical functions

  • f(x) = x^2 + 3x
  • Functions can be used in other expressions: y = f(x).

Sequences and indexed values

  • a_n = n^2 defines a sequence.
  • a_1 evaluates to 1, a_2 to 4, and a_3 to 9.
  • y = a_1*x uses the first term as a number.
  • Sequence indices start at 1.

Variables

  • k = 5
  • y = k*x

Operators

  • + - * / %
  • ^ or **: power
  • ^^: tetration
  • ^^^: pentation
  • !: factorial
  • Implicit multiplication: 2x, 3(x+1), 2pi

Built-in functions

sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, sinh, cosh, tanh, sqrt, cbrt, abs, exp, ln, log, log2, log10, floor, ceil, round, trunc, sign, min, max, pow, hypot, atan2, mod, gcd, lcm, nCr, nPr, mean, median, sum, product, tetration, pentation.
